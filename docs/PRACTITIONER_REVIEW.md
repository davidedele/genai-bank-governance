# Practitioner review worksheet

Purpose: test whether the framework captures the information a governance professional needs, without claiming product-market fit or validated risk reduction.

Ask reviewers to assess anonymized synthetic cases independently before showing the tool's outputs. Do not collect confidential banking examples or identifiable employee prompts.

## Questions to ask

1. What additional facts would you need before deciding whether this activity may proceed?
2. Which categories are ambiguous, and which fields would you refuse to infer from the employee's description?
3. Which risk/control links are wrong or incomplete for your organization's policy?
4. Who should own, review and independently assess this use? Which responsibilities does the prototype conflate?
5. Which changes should trigger reassessment: new data, a new model, expanded retrieval, or a different use of the output?
6. Would this replace an existing step, or add another questionnaire? Where would it enter your current process?

Keep an anonymized disagreement log. Separate taxonomy problems from extraction mistakes and bank-specific policy choices. Two reviewers agreeing with a few examples is useful feedback, not an accuracy certification. Commercial discovery should separately establish the current workflow, unmet need, adoption constraints and buyer.
